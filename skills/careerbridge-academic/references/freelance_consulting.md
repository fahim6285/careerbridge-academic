# Freelance and academic consulting proposals

## Scope
Legitimate professional services: research support, literature/search assistance, data auditing, curriculum design, educational content, academic formatting, technical editing, coaching, simulation assistance and document preparation. Respect intellectual-property and academic-integrity constraints. Do not facilitate ghostwritten assessed student work, fake credentials, fake experiments or fabricated exam content.

## Professional-bid template
Opening: direct understanding of client need. Capability: one or two supported competencies, not copied boilerplate or inflated qualifications. Method: verification/cross-checking against authoritative sources, human review, delivery QC. Scope/deliverables: concrete files and timeline only when feasible. Close: a relevant question or succinct offer.

## Platform and ownership controls
Match *the account holder's* experience and pronouns; never assume another person is a novice because their account is used. Count visible characters including spaces against explicit hard caps (e.g. 1500). Do not promise guaranteed approval, plagiarism-score outcomes, acceptance, review removal or a particular publication decision. Respect platform pricing, tax, GST/TCS and payment policies; check current official laws when legal/tax questions arise, don't guess legal obligations from memory.

## Delivery
One concise copy-ready bid. Provide exact character count if cap exists. If bid amount is requested, explain fee assumptions and choose an amount only with user preference or a clearly labeled example; distinguish proposal text from actual bid submission.
