# Role-specific documents and academic statements

## Cover letter
Base on official role title/department/university/posting ID, verified candidate attributes and specific fit. Preferred order: respectful opening and targeted role; concise research/teaching fit; selected concrete documented accomplishments; service/leadership/industry/mentorship relevance; why that institution/department; professional closing and supporting documents as warranted. Do not use generic praise of university prestige unless verified and relevant. Never invent citation counts, publication ranks, h-index, patents, funding or appointments.

## Teaching statement
Describe pedagogical approach with verifiable examples: course development, learner engagement, formative assessment, laboratory/pedagogy practice, inclusive student support, measured outcomes if documented, and future alignment. Avoid promising prior teaching outcomes not supported by evidence. Match local context (course vs module, credit framework, practical/lab teaching, accreditation) when relevant.

## Research statement
Write a career-facing summary of established lines of work and credible future agenda, differentiating prior achievements, ongoing work and plans. No methods/results fabricated and no turning speculative milestones into funded projects. This is NOT a manuscript, literature review or grant proposal; delegate those to R&A AGENT.

## Other materials
Selection-criteria statement, leadership/service narrative, professional bio, one-page research profile, additional information, statement of purpose/motivation, referee contact list (only with authorization), teaching portfolio index, letter of interest and follow-up. Produce versions only if asked or job advert requires them. Follow explicit word/character limits and document naming patterns; editable DOCX/PDF if tools permit.

## Strict editing discipline
When given an existing draft, preserve correct content and organization. Change only the parts needed to align with requested role and user's correction. No 'as supplied' meta-commentary, editor notes, AI provenance claims or hidden placeholders in final copy. Use discreet internal questions only for truly blocking issues.
