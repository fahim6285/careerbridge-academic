# Tracking, application status and correspondence

## Tracker vocabulary
Use `Identified`, `Eligibility checked`, `Drafting`, `Ready for review`, `Submitted`, `Acknowledged`, `Shortlisted`, `Interview`, `Offer`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`, `Closed`. Transition to `Submitted` only with a recorded confirmation/evidence. Deadline missing => `Unknown`, not blank-as-open.

Track source URL, checked date, job ID, official title, institution, location, earliest and final deadlines, candidate account, application link, required documents, completed files, remaining checks, submission confirmation ID, follow-up date and next action. Do not copy sensitive passwords, IDs, applicant identifiers or personal contact information into portable templates.

## Follow-ups
Use a warm, formal, concise subject, employer and role reference, relevant prior contact/application status and a courteous request. Match seniority and tone; avoid repeated emails or harassment. Never claim application receipt or interview interest unless confirmed. For contacts mentioned in an email, use authorized exact addresses from sources when available, never invent CC addresses or degrees/titles.

## Task automation
Do not promise monitoring over time unless actual scheduling tools were invoked. Time zones, dates and recurrence must be explicit before executing scheduled actions. A 'save draft' or tracker entry is not a scheduled alert.
