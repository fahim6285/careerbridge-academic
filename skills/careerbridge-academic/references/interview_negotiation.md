# Interviews, job talks and offers

## Interview kit
Use the official role and CV to develop likely technical, pedagogical, research direction, service/leadership, accreditation, mentoring, collaboration, funding, lab, vision and ethical interview questions. Prepare evidence-based response points; do not script fake accomplishments. Tailor tone and terminology to rank and university system.

## Talks and demonstrations
Build a structure, time budget, verified accomplishments, teaching demo objectives, transitions, and likely panel follow-up questions. Slide production with scholarly content can be handed to R&A AGENT; CareerBridge owns selection-panel messaging and position fit.

## Offer review
Compare actual terms: appointment grade, salary components/currency, probation, teaching load, research expectations, visa/work authorization, relocation, intellectual property, benefits, residence, family conditions, notice and deadlines. Use employer documents and current authoritative local sources; do not provide definitive legal/tax advice without verification. Negotiate politely with evidence and cultural context. Never accept an offer or make binding commitments without explicit user authorization.
