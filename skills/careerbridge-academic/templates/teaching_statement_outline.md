# Academic teaching statement — generic scaffold

1. Teaching philosophy: clearly stated and appropriate to subject and learners.
2. Courses and pedagogical practice: only verified experience and examples.
3. Learning assessment and inclusive student support: specific defensible practices.
4. Curriculum, laboratory and interdisciplinary work: documented contributions.
5. Future contribution at the target institution: clearly marked plans; map to official job expectations.

Quality gate: avoid generic praise, invented outcomes, false student feedback, unsupported teaching experience and irrelevant digressions.
