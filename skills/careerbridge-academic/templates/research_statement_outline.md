# Academic research statement — generic scaffold

1. Research identity, expertise and relevance to target department.
2. Completed work: evidence-supported contributions and selected credible outputs.
3. Current activity: accurate status; no fabricated results, collaborators, grants or acceptance.
4. Future research direction: feasible objectives marked as future, not accomplished.
5. Expected department-level fit: infrastructure, teaching, partnerships and recruitment criteria drawn from real sources.

This is a career-facing statement, not a manuscript or funder proposal.
